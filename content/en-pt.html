Continental is a leading tire manufacturer and industry specialist that develops and produces sustainable, safe and convenient solutions for automotive manufacturers as well as industrial and end customers worldwide. Founded in 1871, the company generated sales of €19.7 billion in 2025 and currently employs around 78,000 people in 54 countries and markets.

About Continental in Portugal

As Continental in Portugal, we enable value creation and growth by providing an application portfolio which is best-in-class in the tire industry. Our plant in Lousado produces passenger car tires, with an annual production capacity of around 18 million tires. Over the past years, Continental has invested in Lousado around one billion Euros into increasing the production capability, expanding high-performance tire production and ramping-up the production of  off-the-road tires in Lousado. In 2023 a new IT hub for Digital Solutions, Data Science and Supply Chain Management was launched in Portugal - Continental Solution Center. These investments are aligned with Continental's Tire Sector long-term vision and strategy.

Our Locations in Portugal

Continental Mabor, Lousado (CMIP)

Continental Mabor started in July 1990 as a joint venture between Mabor (40%) and Continental (60%). In 1993, Continental acquired the remaining capital. Since its start, the plant expanded its facilities to 799,150 m². Nowadays it has a covered area of more than 314,000 m² and also increased tire production from 5,000 to over 50,000 tires per day. Continental Mabor manufactures passenger and off-the-road tires and has in its facilities an Evaluation and Testing Center for several tires. There is also a recently opened technology hub in Lousado which develops solutions that are replicated across the Group's tire plants.

Continental-Indústria Têxtil do Ave, Lousado (C-ITA)

Continental-Indústria Têxtil do Ave produces textile solutions for various industries, specializing in fabrics and cords for tires, and knitted fabrics for artificial leather applications. Continental-ITA's vision demonstrates where it came from (textiles) and at the same time the willingness to go where the future takes it; its mission focuses on creating value, in various forms, for all stakeholders.

Continental Pneus, Lousado (CPP)

Continental Pneus, Lousado is responsible for the sales of Continental Group tires in Portugal for both OEM and replacement market for PLT, 4x4s, commercial, and industrial vehicles. With a close approach to its customers, the company has positioned itself as a market benchmark in terms of profitability and sustained presence, providing products and services able to create value for its Business Partners. With safe mobility as a fundamental base, the company carries out various initiatives under the Vision Zero project, for a future without accidents.

Continental Lemmerz, Palmela (CLCA)

Continental Lemmerz, founded on September 27, 1994, is a company specialized in tire/wheel assembly, supplying them on a Just In Time basis, with a modern, fully automated assembly line with a capacity of over 1,000,000 wheels per year (from rim 16" to 21"). The barcode system ensures complete traceability of the process from component's reception to shipment. Owned by Continental Mabor and by Schedl Automotive System Service Auslandsbeteiligungsgesellschaft mbH, its sole client is VW Autoeuropa.

Continental Solution Center, Lousado (CSC)

Continental Solution Center aims to boost tire solution digitalization, optimizing both production processes and commercial operations. Incorporating artificial intelligence creates improvements, it can enhance final finishing activities, by detecting deviations almost imperceptible to the human eye. Another CSC’s focus area is tire management, such as the ContiConnect solution, that provides the digital monitoring of tire metrics (pressure, temperature and mileage) which enables specific action recommendations.

Information for Suppliers

Continental is constantly searching not only for new potential clients, but also for new potential suppliers and business partners. Within Continental a big range of purchasing activities are centralized and executed by the Corporate Purchasing department. We are divided into Production Material and Non-Production Material, with teams working in different countries all over the world. 

Our Vision: Our vision is to fuel Continental's success through purchasing performance.

Our Mission: Our mission is to provide market expertise and sustainable purchasing solutions to source goods and services competitively for all of Continental. Our customers value us, because we create value.

Our Behavior: Continental is committed to honesty and integrity with respect to our entire business conduct towards employees, customers, suppliers, competitors and other stakeholders. We expect all our suppliers and business partners to adhere to the same level of commitment we implement internally. 

The Sustainability Management function within Corporate Purchasing ensures, that we work together in creating a better and more sustainable world for future generations. This function works close together with our Corporate Sustainability department.

We as Purchasing trust that compliance is a key factor of satisfying requirements and expectations of our suppliers. We request our suppliers who are aware of illegal and dubious business activities involving Continental and its employees to use our Integrity Hotline. To disregard and ignore such behaviour is not acceptable.

Members and Remuneration of the Executive Board

Since it was founded in 1871, Continental has developed into one of the world’s leading technology companies. In its early days, Continental produced parts made of soft rubber, such as hoof buffers for horses. From the outset, the strategic focus has always been on diversity and innovation. Shortly after its foundation, the company manufactured more than 60,000 items – from the initial hoof guard and hard rubber parts for various applications to rubberized fabrics for airplanes, airships and hot air balloons.

Early on, Continental brought numerous innovations to the market, such as pneumatic tires for bicycles in 1892 and tread tires for cars in 1904. As a result, Continental has become known worldwide in the past century. Today, we are one of the world’s key suppliers for mobility. With the experience we have gained over 150 years and our expertise across the company, we will continue to set the technological benchmark and play a leading role in shaping the transformation in the automotive industry.

Continental Tires

Tires

With its premium portfolio in the car, truck, bus, two-wheel and specialty tire segment, the Tires group sector stands for innovative solutions in tire technology. For specialist dealers and fleet management, Tires offers digital tire monitoring and tire management systems, in addition to other services, with the aim of keeping fleets mobile and increasing their efficiency. With its tires, Continental makes a significant contribution to safe, efficient and environmentally friendly mobility.
