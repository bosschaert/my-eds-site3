Your Career Level and Our Entry Opportunities

As a globally active company, we offer potential candidates a wide variety of entry opportunities. Assign yourself to one of the groups listed below and receive valuable information for your next career step.

Go straight to the job portal

Experienced Professionals

Would you like to change your career? Do you want to take it to the next level? Or are you looking for new challenges? Join the team and enjoy attractive benefits, structured onboarding, and much more.

Students and Graduates

Would you like to gain practical experience while studying? Or have you completed your degree and want to get started? Continental offers internships, student traineeships, theses, diverse trainee programs, or direct entry for ambitious and talented individuals.

Apprenticeship and Dual Studies

Your school days are coming to an end? Do you want to turn your interests into a career? Or do something practical right away in your next phase of life? At Continental, you can complete an apprenticeship or a dual study program in many different areas of the company.
