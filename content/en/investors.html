Investor Relations

Continental is a leading tire manufacturer and industry specialist that develops and produces safe and convenient solutions for automotive manufacturers as well as industrial and end customers worldwide. Founded in 1871, the company generated sales of €19.7 billion in 2025 and currently employs around 76,000 people in 54 countries and markets.

Porsche Chooses Continental Tires

September 29, 2026 Continental has received two new approvals to supply original-equipment tires for Porsche models. The automotive manufacturer has approved both the SportContact 7 and the new SportContact 7 Force for the Panamera G3 II. The SportContact 7 Force is a high-performance addition to Continental’s summer tire line. Porsche also recommends the WinterContact TS 860 S for the Cayenne E4 during the colder months.

Read the press release

Continental Sells mycar Tyre & Auto to Tuhu

September 24, 2026 Continental has announced the sale of its subsidiary mycar Tyre & Auto, one of the largest tyre, automotive service and repair chains in Australia, to Tuhu, a leading Chinese automotive service platform. The transaction is based on an enterprise value of 403 million Australian dollars (approx. 250 million euros). The transaction marks another step in Continental’s strategic focus on its core business to develop and manufacture tyres.

Read the press release

Continental Expands Portfolio of Large and High-Performance Tires

September 16, 2026 Continental has significantly expanded its global portfolio of ultra-high-performance (UHP) tires – tires measuring 18 inches and above – over the past five years and plans to strengthen this segment further in the future. By the end of 2027, the company will add more than 650 new tire sizes across all tire lines, for both original equipment on new cars and the replacement market. By expanding its portfolio, the tire manufacturer is responding to growing demand for high-performance tires designed for larger, heavier and more powerful vehicles, as well as electrified drive systems, while laying the foundation for further profitable growth in this segment.
