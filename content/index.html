OUR BALBRIGGAN

Balbriggan is set to be transformed; its rejuvenation plan sets out an ambitious new vison shaped by the towns people. The total investment in physical, infrastructural improvements between 2020-2030 is currently projected at €54 – €57 million

The Our Balbriggan Rejuvenation programme is led by Fingal County Council’s Economic, Community & Cultural Development.  A Leadership Group chaired by Dr. David Begg is responsible for stewardship and progress on all aspects of the Rejuvenation Plan.
