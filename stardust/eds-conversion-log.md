## Decisions
- **Mobile-only line breaks (`<br class="u-ds-sp">`).** Class attributes on `<br>` do not survive the pipeline. Convention for every authored text that carries them: an always-on break becomes a paragraph boundary (one `<p>` per desktop line), a mobile-only break stays a plain `<br>`; the owning block/section CSS hides `br` at ≥ the desktop breakpoint. Applies to the intro (default content, `.section.intro`), the footer contact copy and the footer address. Content-diff will flag the paragraph split as a JOIN/SPLIT 🟡 (#87), accepted.
- **Slide-1 pin.** The live hero was gated pinned to slide 1; the carousel renders slide 1 first, autoplay from the block JS (R-01/R-02 in the register apply unchanged).
- **GTM (dynamic rows 5–7, 10)** stays pending the owner's decision; not loaded on the new origin in this run.
- **Fonts:** Kulim Park 400/600 + Noto Sans JP — same public faces as the prototype (Google Fonts, `styles/fonts.css`, never in `head.html`).
- **Links:** internal hrefs root-relative via `localize-links.mjs --source-host sorra.jp`; the targets outside this run (works, journal, about…) stay as root-relative paths that will 404 until those pages migrate — recorded as out-of-scope for E2.
