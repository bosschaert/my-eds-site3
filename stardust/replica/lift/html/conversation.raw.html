Conversation

If something sparked a thought, the door is open.

This is the sitting room. If something here stayed with you — write to me. A story you noticed, a question, an idea, or a piece of work that might benefit from another chair.

Stories have always travelled by the most intimate means available — from the fireside to the written word, from letter to radio, from radio to the screen, and now to WhatsApp, where real conversations happen today. Some of the stories in this house are written to live there — to be read, shared and discussed the way stories have always travelled.

And if a story stays with you and you think of someone who might need it — gift it. Every story carries a quiet option to send it to someone you care about. That is how stories have always moved — from one person to another, with care.

I read everything. I answer personally, and without hurry.
