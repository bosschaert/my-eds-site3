Welcome to my home.

Rooms for stories, books and a life observed closely.

I have spent decades moving through very different chapters of work — organisations, functions, roles, geographies. A lifelong student of human behaviour, I have honed and polished my gift of observation and intuition — studying the silences, the noise and the outbursts.

Today I follow my passion — working with nature, and writing about what all those chapters have taught me.

My sincere and heartfelt wish is to give — to transmit — in the hope that it helps a fertile mind like yours to do better, to excel, and to bring out its very best.
